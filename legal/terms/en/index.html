1. What the App Does

  • The App lets you set down short notes and URLs one at a time (each a "slip"), and open them again after time has passed.
  • When you set down a slip, our server uses AI to give it a title and to pick earlier slips about the same thing. When you open a slip with a URL, the App shows short pointers to look at before you go and read it (what it is about, things to look for, and questions for after reading). They are not a summary of the page; the original at the link is where you check the content. For these purposes our server accesses the URL to retrieve its title and text. The retrieval is performed by our server, not by your device.
  • On Android, you can pass text and URLs to the App from another app's share menu. What we receive is held on your device and becomes a slip the next time you open the App. This feature is not available on iOS.
  • If you set a time on a slip, the App schedules a notification on your device. Notifications run entirely on your device; we do not send them to you individually.
  • Slips, and what the App generates for them, are stored in a database on your device. Our server does not store the content. There is no cross-device sync and no cloud backup. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • The App is provided AS-IS. We make no express or implied warranties about availability, accuracy, or the content of anything the AI generates.
  • There is no advertising in the App, and there is no account sign-up.

2. What Is Free, and What "Izure Pro" Unlocks

The following are available at no charge:

  • Setting down slips, reading them again, adding to them, and deleting them. Slips do not become unavailable because of how long ago they were set down.
  • Opening a slip with a URL to see the pointers before reading. For links whose content we cannot see (§4), the App shows questions for you instead.
  • "Seeds" for slips set down in your own words, and picking earlier slips about the same thing when you set down a slip.
  • Stacking slips on another slip to group them (a "stack").
  • Choosing when a slip should notify you. At no charge, the choices are limited to the presets offered by the App (in an hour, tonight, tomorrow).
  • Receiving content from the share menu (Android), and deleting the data held on your device.

Purchasing the paid plan "Izure Pro" (the "Plan") unlocks the following:

  • Ask to find: type what you are looking for in a sentence, and the App shows matching slips from those you have set down, each with a one-line reason.
  • Finding related articles from outside for a stack: the App searches the web based on the stack's titles and similar, and shows up to three articles it has confirmed can be opened, each with one line showing how it connects to the stack. It also shows, for each slip in a stack, one line on how it connects to the stack's base. Searches have a monthly limit.
  • Export: turn the slips you have set down and the words you wrote into plain text or Markdown and hand them to another app through your device's share feature.
  • Monthly review: shows the words you wrote in a month, grouped by topic.
  • Setting the notification time freely, by date and time.

Retrieval of pages and posts depends on what the source site or service makes publicly available and on any instruction declining retrieval (Privacy Policy §3.3). Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok links are not retrieved. If a source changes its specifications, what can be retrieved may change without notice. We do not guarantee what can be retrieved, but we will update this section once we become aware of such a change.

To keep server load and cost within reasonable limits, we set caps on how many times the AI-backed features may be used per short interval, per day, and per calendar month. The caps differ depending on whether you hold the Plan, and are set higher for Plan holders. When a cap is reached, that feature may be unavailable until the relevant period ends. We may change the values of these caps; where a change is disadvantageous to Plan holders, we follow the procedure in §13.
