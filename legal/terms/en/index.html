1. What the App Does

  • The App lets you set down short notes and URLs one at a time (each a "slip"), and open them again after time has passed.
  • When you open a slip, our server generates a piece of writing with the help of AI. If the slip contains a URL, our server accesses that URL to retrieve its title and text and uses them as material for that writing. The retrieval is performed by our server, not by your device.
  • On Android, you can pass text and URLs to the App from another app's share menu. What we receive is held on your device and becomes a slip the next time you open the App. This feature is not available on iOS.
  • If you set a time on a slip, the App schedules a notification on your device. Notifications run entirely on your device; we do not send them to you individually.
  • Slips and their generated writing are stored in a database on your device. Our server does not store the content. There is no cross-device sync and no cloud backup. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • The App is provided AS-IS. We make no express or implied warranties about availability, accuracy, or the content of anything the AI generates.
  • There is no advertising in the App, and there is no account sign-up.

2. What Is Free, and What "Izure Pro" Unlocks

The following are available at no charge:

  • Setting down slips, reading them again, adding to them, and deleting them.
  • Opening a slip to generate a piece of writing. At no charge, the App shows that writing up to its first section.
  • Scheduling notifications on a slip, receiving content from the share menu (Android), and deleting the data held on your device.

Purchasing the paid plan "Izure Pro" (the "Plan") unlocks the following:

  • The remaining sections of the writing, which are folded away at no charge.
  • Combining several slips into a single piece of writing (a "stack"). Stacks cannot be created at no charge.

To keep server load and cost within reasonable limits, we set caps on how many times the AI-backed features may be used per short interval, per day, and per calendar month. The caps differ depending on whether you hold the Plan, and are set higher for Plan holders. When a cap is reached, that feature may be unavailable until the relevant period ends. We may change the values of these caps; where a change is disadvantageous to Plan holders, we follow the procedure in §10.
